qlab cheatsheets

Update rules, schedules, and clipping paired with the optimizer.

Optimizers

Menu — how to pick

LLM pretrain: AdamW + warmup + cosine. Large-batch vision: SGD + momentum. Memory-bound: Adafactor or 8-bit Adam. Unstable Adam steps → see Failure modes (adam_collapse).

SGD 
θ ← θ − η ∇L(θ)

Low memory; sensitive to η and batch size. No per-parameter scaling.

SGD + momentum / Nesterov 
v ← μv + ∇L ;  θ ← θ − η v

Default for many ResNet recipes. Nesterov: gradient at look-ahead point.

Adam 
m ← β₁m + (1−β₁)g ;  v ← β₂v + (1−β₂)g²
θ ← θ − η · m̂ / (√v̂ + ε)

Adaptive steps; transformers default before decoupled decay. Reddi et al.: some convex failures.

AdamW 
θ ← θ − η ( m̂/(√v̂+ε) + λθ )

Decoupled weight decay (Loshchilov & Hutter). BERT/GPT-class pretrain and fine-tune.

RMSprop / Adadelta

RMSprop: EMA of g² scales steps. Adadelta: no global η. RL and older CV stacks.

AMSGrad

max over past v̂ before denominator (Reddi et al. 2018). Drop-in when Adam diverges on paper examples.

Adafactor

Factored second moments → lower optimizer memory (T5-scale training).

Lion / LAMB

Lion: sign(momentum of grad). LAMB: layer-wise trust ratio for huge batch BERT (You et al.).

Schedules & clipping
  • Linear warmup — Post-LN transformers (Xiong et al. 2020).
  • Cosine / WSD — LLM decay schedules.
  • Grad clip — global norm τ (Pascanu et al. 2013).
  • Weight decay — tune λ with AdamW; too strong hurts rank (see failure modes).
