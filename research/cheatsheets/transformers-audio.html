qlab cheatsheets

ASR, audio understanding, music/speech generation.

Transformers — audio & speech

Whisper-style ASR
  • Log-mel → conv stem (time downsample) → transformer encoder
  • Decoder: causal self-attn + cross-attn to audio → text tokens
  • Multitask: transcribe, translate, language id (optional heads)
Audio classification / tags
  • AST — mel as patch grid; ViT encoder
  • BEATs / AudioMAE — masked acoustic modeling
  • Pooling + classifier head; fine-tune or linear probe
Audio + LLM
  • Audio tower (Whisper enc, BEATs, etc.) → projector → LLM tokens
  • Q-Former or MLP connector; chat over sound/events
  • Align on paired speech–text or weak labels
Neural codecs (token front-end)
  • EnCodec / SoundStream: CNN enc → RVQ codes → dec waveform
  • Transformers model discrete codes, not raw samples
  • Enables long-context music modeling at manageable seq length
Music & audio generation
  • AR — AudioLM, MusicLM, MusicGen on codec tokens; decoder-only
  • Diffusion — AudioLDM, Stable Audio on spectrogram/latent
  • Conditioning: text embeds, chroma, melody tokens
TTS
  • Two-stage: text → mel (transformer enc–dec) → neural vocoder
  • Single-stage AR: discrete speech tokens (VALL-E / Voicebox class)
  • Speaker/prosody: global embeds, LoRA/adapters
