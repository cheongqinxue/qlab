Failure modes

Mode index (qlab ids)
  • explode — gradient explosion
  • vanish — gradient vanishing
  • adam_collapse — Adam instability
  • attention_sink — attention sink
  • rank_collapse — rank / spectral collapse (weights)
  • logit_collapse — logit / output collapse
  • representation_collapse — representation collapse
  • layer_grad_imbalance — layer-wise gradient imbalance
  • no_warmup, decay_norm_bias, bad_mlm_mask — MLM best-practice pathologies
How to read this sheet

Each mode: what (literature term), spot (metrics), mitigate, ref. Long-form notes: tutorials/pathologies/doc/failure-modes/*.md.

Gradient explosion (explode)

What: ‖∇‖ or loss spikes → NaN (Pascanu et al.).

Spot: train/grad_norm, train/update_to_weight_ratio.

Mitigate: clip-by-norm, lower η, warmup, Pre-LN / residuals (He et al.).

Gradient vanishing (vanish)

What: Jacobian product < 1 → weak updates deep in stack or in time.

Spot: flat loss; depth-wise grad norm decay.

Mitigate: residuals, LayerNorm, LSTM/GRU (Hochreiter & Schmidhuber).

Adam instability (adam_collapse)

What: step η·m̂/(√v̂+ε) spikes; loss diverges (Reddi; Zhang; Bai et al.).

Spot: train/adam_second_moment/*, adam_max_r, g²/v spikes.

Mitigate: raise ε, clip, AMSGrad, moment reset; tune AdamW.

Attention sink (attention_sink)

What: peaked attention on few keys/tokens (Darcet et al.; Xiao et al.).

Spot: attention_max_weight, attention_head_imbalance.

Mitigate: ViT registers; streaming sink KV; init fixes.

Rank collapse (rank_collapse)

What: weight W low effective rank (σ₁ ≫ σ₂…).

Not: neural collapse (Papyan) or dimensional collapse (Jing et al. SSL).

Spot: train/effective_rank/*. Mitigate: reduce weight decay.

Logit collapse (logit_collapse)

What: softmax too peaked; H(p) falls (Szegedy et al. label smoothing motivation).

Spot: train/logit_entropy, ECE.

Mitigate: label smoothing, temperature scaling, warmup.

Representation collapse (representation_collapse)

What: features/embeddings lose diversity; contrastive trivial f(x)=const (SimCLR).

Spot: train/embedding_anisotropy, cosine mass near 1.

Mitigate: unfreeze body; negatives in InfoNCE.

Layer grad imbalance (layer_grad_imbalance)

What: embedding/head vs body update mismatch (ULMFiT discriminative LRs).

Spot: train/update_ratio/* by group.

Mitigate: per-block learning rates.

Related literature terms
  • Neural collapse — late supervised ETF geometry (Papyan et al. 2020).
  • Dimensional collapse — SSL covariance rank (Jing et al. 2022).
  • Dead ReLU — saturated units; change activation or η.
  • Catastrophic forgetting — sequential tasks; not a single-run spike.
MLM best-practice modes
  • no_warmup — Post-LN early instability (Xiong et al.).
  • decay_norm_bias — decay on norm/bias.
  • bad_mlm_mask — wrong mask ratio.
