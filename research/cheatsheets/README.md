**Open:** [GitHub Pages](https://cheongqinxue.github.io/qlab/index.html) (or [index.html](index.html) locally). Side nav is grouped by category.

Nav categories: **Training & optimization** · **Testing** · **DevOps** · **MLOps** · **Transformers** · **Databases** · **Security** · **RAG** · **Agents** · **Interview prep** (see `js/cheatsheet-nav.js`).

**Agents track:** [Local model eval matrix](https://cheongqinxue.github.io/qlab/agents-model-eval.html) (`agents/model_eval` results; regenerate with `export_cheatsheet_data.py`).

**Authoring:** see [PATTERN.md](PATTERN.md). Nav entries: edit [`js/cheatsheet-nav.js`](js/cheatsheet-nav.js).
